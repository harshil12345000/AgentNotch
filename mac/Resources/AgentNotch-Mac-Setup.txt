NOTCH FOR MAC - SETUP
=====================

AgentNotch needs macOS 13 or later. Setup takes about a minute.


THE EASY WAY (recommended)
--------------------------
Open the Terminal app, paste this line and press Return:

    curl -fsSL https://agentnotch-six.vercel.app/install.sh | sh

That is all. AgentNotch appears at the top of your screen. You can skip the rest
of this file and delete the zip you downloaded.


THE MANUAL WAY (using the zip you downloaded)
---------------------------------------------
1. Double-click AgentNotch-mac.zip to unzip it. You get the AgentNotch app.

2. Right-click (or Control-click) AgentNotch and choose Open.
   Do not double-click it the first time: macOS blocks apps that are not
   notarized by Apple, and a double-click only shows a warning.

3. In the dialog, click Open.

   If there is no Open button (macOS 15 and later):
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the line that says AgentNotch was blocked.
     - Click Open Anyway, then confirm.
     - Open AgentNotch again.

4. AgentNotch moves itself into your Applications folder (the one in your home
   folder) and starts. You can delete the zip and the unzipped copy.


WHAT HAPPENS ON FIRST LAUNCH
----------------------------
- AgentNotch appears at the top centre of the screen. Move the pointer onto it
  to open it.
- It connects itself to Claude Code and Codex if they are installed. Your
  own hooks are kept, and a backup of the settings file is saved.
- It is set to start when you log in. You can turn that off from the AgentNotch
  icon in the menu bar.
- Sessions that were already open show up at once. They report live
  details after you restart them.


PROMPTS YOU MAY SEE
-------------------
- "AgentNotch wants to use your confidential information stored in Claude
  Code-credentials": this lets AgentNotch show your usage limits. Click Always
  Allow, or Deny if you do not want usage shown. Everything else works
  either way.
- Notifications: allow them if you want a banner when an agent needs you.
- Codex asks once to trust the new hooks. Type /hooks in Codex and accept.
- Keep Awake asks for your Mac password, because stopping a closed lid from
  sleeping the Mac is a system-wide setting.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Hide or show AgentNotch: Control + \
- Menu bar icon: show or hide, clear finished sessions, quit.
- AgentNotch docks at the top of the screen only on the Mac.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "AgentNotch is damaged and can't be opened": open Terminal and run
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/AgentNotch.app
  then open AgentNotch again. Or use the easy way at the top of this file.
- Nothing appears: look for the AgentNotch icon in the menu bar and choose
  Show / Hide, or press Control + \.
- No sessions listed: start a Claude Code or Codex session. Only sessions
  that are open right now are shown.


REMOVE NOTCH
------------
In Terminal:

    ~/Applications/AgentNotch.app/Contents/MacOS/AgentNotch --uninstall-hooks
    ~/Applications/AgentNotch.app/Contents/MacOS/AgentNotch --uninstall-hooks --codex

Then quit AgentNotch from its menu bar icon and delete:

    ~/Applications/AgentNotch.app
    ~/Library/Application Support/AgentNotch
